## 2. Project Idea

Hibiscus (known in Egypt as *karkadeh*) is part of everyday Egyptian life. This project aims to build awareness of Egyptian hibiscus and KADÉ among UK consumers, educate them about how to enjoy it, engage them with the brand and generate interest in its products. It develops a branding and digital marketing strategy for **KADÉ**, a virtual Egyptian hibiscus brand targeting UK consumers, and puts it into practice through a social media content plan and a paid Meta campaign targeting the UK market and audiences.

**Proposed positioning:** accessible premium, certified organic Egyptian hibiscus herbal tea.

**Proposed USP:** *Authentic Egyptian Hibiscus, Made Easy — Tailored to Your Taste.*

Certified organic · Caffeine-free · Sugar-free · Gluten-free · Dairy-free

**Proposed product range (academic proposal):**

| Product | Format | Pack size | Proposed UK price |
|---|---|---|---|
| KADÉ Hibiscus Tea Bags | Tea bags | 20 bags (40 g) | £4.49 |
| KADÉ Hibiscus Tea Bags | Tea bags | 50 bags | £9.99 |
| KADÉ Loose Hibiscus Flowers | Loose flowers | 100 g | £5.99 |
| KADÉ Loose Hibiscus Flowers | Loose flowers | 200 g | £10.99 |

**Marketing mix (4Ps):**

| Element | KADÉ approach |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Premium, single-origin, certified organic 100% pure Egyptian hibiscus, offered as tea bags (20 and 50 bags) and loose flowers (100 g and 200 g), with original KADÉ branding and premium packaging designs |
| **Price** | Accessible premium pricing from £4.49 to £10.99, benchmarked against competing hibiscus and herbal tea products in the UK |
| **Place** | A phased, digital-first approach: the KADÉ website, social media and Amazon UK first; then specialist tea and health stores, cafés and distributors; and later larger retailers such as Ocado |
| **Promotion** | A 21-post social media content plan across four pillars (awareness, education, engagement and product promotion), a paid Meta campaign targeting UK audiences and a proposed QR code linking consumers to hot and cold hibiscus recipes |

**Scope of the project:**

- UK market research and competitor analysis (herbal tea brands and online retailers)
- Consumer insights from reviews and online communities
- PEST and SWOT analysis
- SMART marketing objectives and KPIs
- Buyer personas (*Sophie Williams* and *James Carter*)
- Marketing mix (4Ps)
- Brand identity: name, logo, colour palette, typography and packaging design
- Brand and content guidelines, including responsible-claims rules
- Social media content plan (21 posts across four content pillars) and execution tracking
- Paid Meta awareness campaign targeting UK audiences

**Key assumption:** organic certification and UK import approvals are assumed to be in place before launch.
